Our Story

Serving sellers. Blessing buyers. Renewing neighborhoods.

We started Capstone because we saw a gap right here in Nelson, Augusta, and Albemarle County. We built it to fill that gap.

Why we exist.

Right here in our three counties, good neighbors were falling through the cracks. Behind on payments. Stuck with a house that wouldn't sell. Dealing with problems most agents don't know how to solve.

We knew we could help. So we built a company that turns those problems into offers.

We love hard cases. The messier, the better. When someone says their property is the one nobody can figure out — that's when we light up. That's why we exist.
