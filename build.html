Why we're different

We're not remodelers.

Buying, renovating and selling houses is the business, not a sideline. Our money buys the materials, and most of the year our calendar is full of our own projects.

A remodeling company's profit comes from the job. Ours comes from selling the house, so we're the ones who pay for a job that drags. That makes us efficient in a way a remodeler never has to be.

Justin, our GC, runs every one of our jobs and he works for nobody else. The trades on your house are the same ones we use on ours.

Availability

Most of the year, we don't take outside work.

Our calendar is full of our own projects. That's why you've probably never seen our sign in a yard that isn't ours.

But a few times a year we come out of a season with the calendar open. When that happens we take on a small number of homeowners and build for them exactly the way we build for ourselves. Same standards, same GC, same finish.

That's where we are right now. We've finished our summer projects and haven't started the next one, so before fall work begins we have room for two or three homeowners.

When the fall projects start, the window closes.

What We Do

We renovate entire houses, so we can do just about anything.

These are our specialties.

  • Kitchen remodels
  • Bath remodels
  • Additions
