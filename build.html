Remodeling

We don't usually work on other people's homes.

Right now we have room for two or three.

Buying, renovating and selling houses is the business, not a sideline. Our money buys the materials. Most of the year our calendar is full of our own projects, which is why you've probably never seen our sign in a yard that isn't ours.

That's also why we move fast. A remodeling company's profit comes from the job. Ours comes from selling the house, so we're the ones who pay for a job that drags. That makes us efficient in a way a remodeler never has to be.

Justin, our GC, runs every one of our jobs and he works for nobody else. The trades on your house are the same ones we use on ours.
